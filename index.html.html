Featured Project

Under The Table

Crime Thriller · Short Film · 1930s London

Under The Table

Collaborative Group Production · Sound Lead

A crime-thriller short film set in 1930s London, designed to build suspense through cinematic storytelling, immersive visuals, and detailed sound design. Inspired by productions like Peaky Blinders and No Country for Old Men, the film centres on a high-stakes gangster poker game.

My primary role focused on sound production and audio editing — supporting dialogue recording, monitoring on-set audio quality, capturing ambient sounds, and leading post-production sound editing.

How the film
came to life

01
Pre-Production Research
Studied sound recording techniques, microphone positioning, audio setup workflows, and watched editing tutorials to understand how sound elevates storytelling.
02
On-Set Recording
Recorded dialogue and live sound during filming while actively monitoring audio quality. Maintained communication with the team throughout the shoot.
03
Post-Production
Removed background noise, synced audio with video, adjusted sound levels, added atmospheric effects and exported the final polished audio mix.
04
Review & Refine
Conducted editing reviews, integrated team feedback, and revised audio to ensure the sound supported the mood and visual rhythm of every scene.

Reflection

Growth through
every challenge

"Working on Under The Table deepened my understanding of how sound supports storytelling, atmosphere, pacing, and emotional tension within film production."
Challenge Overcome
Effective group communication was initially difficult — coordinating tasks and discussing ideas proved challenging early on. By committing to more open, proactive participation and adapting to the group's working style, team dynamics improved significantly, allowing us to finish the project efficiently and collaboratively.
What I'd Do Differently
I would invest more time in planning and sound quality before filming begins. Better pre-production preparation — particularly in audio setup and location scouting — would reduce editing challenges downstream and lead to a more polished final product.
Forward Focus
I'm continuing to develop my sound production and cinematography skills, exploring how music and image can create greater combined impact. I'm also building technical confidence with industry-standard tools through ongoing practice and new projects.
Overall Outcome
Under The Table successfully combined cinematic storytelling, immersive sound design, and collaborative production to create an engaging crime-thriller short film. The project strengthened both my technical abilities and my creative understanding of what film can achieve.
